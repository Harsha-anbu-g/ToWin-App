# Crisis comms — drafted before it's needed, not during

`trust-and-safety` handles the incident; this skill handles what Towinly *says*. For a trust
brand, the response IS the brand moment — companies are judged on the handling, not the event.

## Principles

1. **Fast, honest, human.** Acknowledge quickly with what is *known*; never speculate, never
   minimize, never blame the victim. "We are treating this with the seriousness it deserves"
   + concrete next step + when we'll update.
2. **The affected family hears from Towinly first** — by phone, before any public statement,
   before press can reach them. Nothing is worse than a family learning from a reporter.
3. **One voice.** The founder (or a named spokesperson) speaks; everyone else routes inquiries.
   Statements are approved by founder + counsel — nothing improvised in public.
4. **Brand voice holds under pressure:** calm, plain words, no corporate deflection
   ("we take security seriously" without specifics reads as evasion). Short sentences.
5. **Never contradict the legal record.** Comms and `legal-docs`/counsel review together;
   sympathy is expressed without admitting fault ("our hearts are with the family" is safe;
   "we failed to screen" is counsel's call).

## Pre-drafted templates to maintain (fill-in-the-blank, reviewed by counsel once)

- **Safety incident** (helper harmed elder / elder harmed helper): family call script, partner
  notification (`institutional-partnerships` partners hear before the press), holding statement,
  full statement, internal note.
- **Data breach:** user notice (plain words, what/when/what-we-did/what-you-should-do),
  DPDP Board + CERT-In 6-hour report (`privacy-two-markets` owns the regulator content),
  US state notifications per counsel.
- **Outage:** in-app + status message in elder-plain language ("Towinly is having trouble
  right now. Your account is safe. We expect to be back by …").
- **Press criticism / viral complaint:** respond to the person before the platform; correct
  facts calmly with evidence; never argue in comment threads.

## The first hour (checklist)

1. Facts from the incident log (`trust-and-safety`) — only confirmed items.
2. Affected people called (support/safety human, per those skills).
3. Holding statement adapted, founder + counsel approve, publish where the question is being asked.
4. Partners who touch the affected users notified personally.
5. Update cadence promised and kept — silence after a first statement reads as cover-up.

## After

Post-incident: what happened, what changed — published when user-relevant (trust is rebuilt by
visible correction). Templates updated with what the real event taught. Log kept alongside the
incident record.
