# Institutional partnerships — how elders actually arrive

Elders are not reached by Instagram ads. They're reached through organizations they already
trust, and through their adult children. Partnership work is Towinly's sales motion.

## The partner map

**US:** senior/community centres, public libraries (tech-help hours are a natural fit),
faith communities, **Area Agencies on Aging** (every region has one — federally funded,
mandate includes exactly this), AARP chapters, home-care agencies (Towinly complements, not
replaces, paid care), retirement communities, Meals-on-Wheels-type services.

**India:** Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs), HelpAge India / Agewell Foundation and local
NGOs, senior citizens' associations, temple/church/mosque communities, senior-living operators
(Primus, Ashiana, Columbia Pacific), housing-society WhatsApp groups (see `localization-india` —
WhatsApp is the channel).

**Both:** the *adult children* are a parallel audience — guardian mode is the family's product.
Partners that reach worried children (caregiver support groups, NRI communities for India)
convert as well as elder-facing ones.

## The pitch (safety story first)

Partners' first question is "is this safe?" — lead with the answer: screening
(`background-checks`, claim only what's true), the Trust Ladder's slow mutual-consent design,
family visibility (guardian mode), a phone-reachable human (`elder-support-desk`), and the
incident process (`trust-and-safety`). Then the benefit: company and help for their members,
at their pace. Never lead with technology; lead with "someone shovels the walk and stays for tea."

## Pilot structure (repeatable)

1. One partner, one geography (aligned with `marketplace-liquidity` — helper coverage exists first).
2. An in-person session at the partner's venue: staff or family help elders onboard
   (assisted signup is expected, not a failure); leave large-print one-pagers.
3. A named Towinly contact with a phone number for the partner's staff.
4. Success = elders matched and repeating (marketplace metrics), reported back to the partner
   monthly in plain words. Partners renew on stories and numbers.
5. Written agreement for anything involving data or payment — route through `legal-docs`;
   never pay partners per-signup (elder-trust optics, and some US referral rules).

## Materials this skill produces

Partner one-pager, safety briefing sheet, large-print elder handout (≥16pt, plain words,
brand parchment style), a 10-minute community-talk outline, and the monthly partner report
template. All claims pass the truth rules (`legal-docs`, `background-checks`).
